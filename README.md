<h2>Practical Tips for Getting the Most Out of Each Device</h2>
<p>You've got 10,000 puffs per device. Here's how to make sure you're getting every one of them.</p>
<h3>Store Devices Correctly</h3>
<ul>
<li>Keep sealed devices at room temperature.</li>
<li>Avoid leaving them in a hot car or direct sunlight.</li>
<li>Don't store them in the freezer - cold affects the fill.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Know When a Device Is Genuinely Finished</h3>
<p>The Crystal Prime 10K has a LED indicator that typically flashes or dims when the device is nearing its end. Don't mistake a temporary air bubble for an empty device. Sometimes taking a slower, longer draw sorts the issue.</p>
<h3>Avoid Chain Vaping</h3>
<p>Rapid, back-to-back puffs can heat the coil too fast. Space your draws out slightly. It improves flavour and extends the life of the device.</p>
